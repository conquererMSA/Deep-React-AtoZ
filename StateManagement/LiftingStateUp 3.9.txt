Duita sibling ba children component ke kuno ekta nirdhisto state diye controll korar jonno Lifting State Up theory use kora hoy.
Lifting State Up e parent component e state declare kore child component e prop hisabe pathanu hoy, ete child component parent component er control e cole ase. Etake parent control compnent bola hoy.

When you want to coordinate two components, move their state to their common parent.
Then pass the information down through props from their common parent.
Finally, pass the event handlers down so that the children can change the parent’s state.
It’s useful to consider components as “controlled” (driven by props) or “uncontrolled” (driven by state).
